- The Henge backend: `open_record("henge:<folder>#<name>")` keeps the record in the Henge single-node store or mirror running in `<folder>`, reached with the client alone, behind the same contract as SQLite (`open_henge`, `HengeRecord`); one writer per record, held by a lock in the store's folder, and every write after the commit point checked against the version it expects, so a crash or a second writer never tears the record; it is built and tested for one store: on a mirror it goes on only past writes the store answered to it, but that is not proven yet, so do not rely on it there; a put or sync the store will not take is tried again with a growing pause until the record's timeout, then gives the operation up (`Unknown` once it is committed), and a refusal for good gives up at once; an open, and a read before an operation's commit, is refused with `Unavailable` at once while a store restarts; `ack` and `beat` are synced; the load is resumable, and a load that returned is durable (it ends with a sync), and `HengeFollower` follows the events from another process. henge-store-client is now a dependency, and Python 3.12 the floor
