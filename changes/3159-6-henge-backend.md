- The Henge backend: `open_record("henge:<folder>#<name>")` keeps the record in the Henge single-node store or mirror running in `<folder>`, reached with the client alone, behind the same contract as SQLite (`open_henge`, `HengeRecord`); one writer per record, held by a lock in the store's folder, and every write after the commit point checked against the version it expects, so a crash or a second writer never tears the record; it is built and tested for one store: on a mirror it goes on only past writes the store answered to it, but that is not proven yet, so do not rely on it there; an operation the store will not take gives up at the record's timeout, `Unknown` once it is committed, and at once on a refusal for good, with a growing pause between its tries; `ack` and `beat` are synced; the load is resumable, and `HengeFollower` follows the events from another process. henge-store-client is now a dependency, and Python 3.12 the floor
